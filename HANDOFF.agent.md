- C: Run KiCAD work through a child `claude.exe -p ... --mcp-config` process, never from this app session, and tell that child not to delegate to a subagent — dynamically loaded Konnect toolsets do not reach subagents either, which stalled the first build attempt. Verify its claims with `kicad-cli` rather than trusting its report. See U8 and U12.
- C: Owner decision of 2026-08-04: `C1`, the 100 nF ADC settling capacitor across `R3`, is **not fitted**, and its job moves into firmware. The divider is deliberately high impedance to hold idle draw at about 21 uA, which leaves the ADC looking at a 50 kOhm source; Seeed's own XIAO battery-measurement guide meets the same problem with a 200k/200k divider and answers it by averaging 16 readings. `src/batterymonitor.cpp` now discards one conversion to charge the sample-and-hold and takes the **median** of `BATTERY_ADC_SAMPLES` readings on the ESP32 `BAT_EXTERNAL` path — a median rather than a mean because it drops outliers instead of smearing them in. `BATTERY_ADC_SAMPLES` defaults to 1, which preserves the original single-shot behaviour for every other board, and `platformio.ini` sets it to 15 for both XIAO environments. `pio-test/test_i2c_contract` pins the value at 15 and asserts it is odd, and `batterymonitor.h` static-asserts the same so an even count cannot be configured. Limit worth carrying forward: software filtering removes jitter and spikes but **not** a steady droop; any constant offset still has to be absorbed by the `BATTERY_SHIELD_RESISTANCE` calibration.
- C: The `BAT_EXTERNAL` code path is not compiled by the shipping configuration, because `board-defaults.json` still selects `BAT_INTERNAL` for the USB stage. It was compile-checked by temporarily switching the board's `BATTERY.type` to `BAT_EXTERNAL`, confirming `batterymonitor.cpp` builds, and reverting; do the same after any change to that path rather than assuming a green build covers it.
- C: The firmware follows the wiring: `board-defaults.json` `BOARD_XIAO_ESP32S3` now has `"int": "9"` and `pio-test/test_i2c_contract/test_main.cpp` asserts `PIN_IMU_INT == 9`. Before this it was `4`, even though the Notion guide had claimed since 2026-08-04 that it was `8` — that change had never reached the code. The pin is unread by the current firmware (the LSM6DSV SoftFusion path polls the FIFO), but the `static_assert` is what keeps the define and the solder joint from drifting apart.
- C: Owner decision of 2026-08-04: `IMU_INT1` runs to U1 `D10` / GPIO9 (pin 11), not `D3` / GPIO4, so `D3` is now a no-connect and `D10` is not. GPIO9 is an RTC GPIO, so it stays usable as a future EXT1 motion-wake source. The Notion guide had moved this line to `D9` / GPIO8 on 2026-08-04 to cut wire crossings; the owner chose GPIO9 instead, which Notion already listed as the sanctioned alternative, so the guide was corrected to match. On `docs/wiring-xiao-lsm6dsv.svg` the run now leaves the module, goes over the top of both boards and down the right-hand side into the right column, crossing `BAT_SENSE`, `GND`, and `+3V3`; all three crossings carry hop arcs.
